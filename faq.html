Common questions about how we work, what things cost, and what happens after launch. Can't find what you need? Just reach out.

General

We build custom mobile and web applications and provide dependable hardware and infrastructure support — so one team can cover both the software you run and the systems it runs on.

We're based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, and work with clients both locally and remotely. Most communication happens over call, email or WhatsApp, with on-site visits arranged for hardware work when needed.

We've worked with clients in retail, logistics, hospitality, finance and healthcare. Our process adapts to the industry, not the other way around.

Reach out through our contact form, phone, or WhatsApp with a short description of what you need. We'll follow up to understand your goals before proposing a scope.
