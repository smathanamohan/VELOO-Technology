A small team that treats your systems like our own

Veloo Technology exists to bridge the gap between complex technology and everyday business needs — with engineering, design and support under one roof.

Our story

Veloo Technology started with a simple observation: most small and mid-sized businesses are stuck choosing between a developer who disappears after launch, and a hardware vendor who has never opened their codebase.

We built Veloo to be the team that does both — designing and building the software you need, and keeping the machines and networks it runs on healthy long after the invoice is paid.

Today that means mobile and web applications, IT support, systems integration and infrastructure, delivered by one accountable team instead of three disconnected vendors.
